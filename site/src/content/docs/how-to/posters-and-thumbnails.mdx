Show artwork before playback starts, and preview frames along the timeline while the user seeks.

## Recommended approach

Use the <DocsLink slug="reference/poster">Poster</DocsLink> component for initial artwork. It layers over the media and gets out of the way when playback starts.

## How it works

- Poster is a player component, not the native `poster` attribute, so you can style it, transition it, and compose it with other UI. It reads playback state to know when to hide.
- Timeline previews come from a storyboard: a `kind="metadata"` text track labeled `thumbnails` whose WebVTT cues map time ranges to image URLs (optionally with sprite coordinates). The <DocsLink slug="reference/feature-text-tracks">text track feature</DocsLink> exposes these as `thumbnailCues` and `thumbnailTrackSrc`.
- The <DocsLink slug="reference/thumbnail">Thumbnail</DocsLink> component renders the storyboard frame for a given `time`; pair it with the time slider's pointer position for seek previews.

## Availability and constraints

- Pair `preload="none"` with a poster so the player isn't a black box before playback. See <DocsLink slug="how-to/sources-and-loading">Sources and Loading</DocsLink>.
- Thumbnails require a storyboard asset generated alongside the media; the player doesn't extract frames from the video itself.
- Cross-origin storyboards need CORS headers, and `crossOrigin` on the media element for the track to load.
- Storyboard images download on demand; oversized sprite sheets hurt seek-preview responsiveness more than they help quality.

## Common variations

### Seek preview on the time slider

Combine <DocsLink slug="reference/time-slider">TimeSlider</DocsLink> with Thumbnail so scrubbing shows the frame under the pointer. See the <DocsLink slug="reference/thumbnail">Thumbnail reference</DocsLink> for the slider-integrated pattern and the storyboard VTT format, including sprite-sheet cues.

### Poster from the media service

Video platforms generate poster and storyboard assets for you — for Mux, a thumbnail URL and a storyboard VTT per asset — so you rarely need to create these by hand.

## Troubleshooting

### The poster never hides

Poster reads playback state; confirm playback actually started (see <DocsLink slug="how-to/autoplay">Autoplay</DocsLink> for why it might not have).

### Thumbnails don't appear while scrubbing

Confirm the storyboard track is `kind="metadata"` with `label="thumbnails"`, the VTT loads (network panel), and cross-origin assets have CORS headers with `crossOrigin` set on the media element.

### Thumbnails are blurry or slow

Regenerate the storyboard with a larger frame size, or split giant sprite sheets into smaller images.
